**Subject:** Enquiry: historical corporate-action data, coverage and provenance (no purchase at this stage)

---

Dear BSE Information Products team,

We are evaluating historical corporate-action data for research on BSE/NSE-listed equities. This is an information request only; we are not placing an order. Could you please let us know:

1. **Event coverage:** which corporate-action types your "Corporate Action via Internet" and historical corporate data products include: bonus, split/sub-division, consolidation, rights (with terms), mergers/amalgamations, demergers/schemes of arrangement, capital reduction, and any others.
2. **Historical periods:** the earliest date available for each event type, and whether coverage is continuous from that date (your product page mentions historical corporate data "since 2006").
3. **Completeness:** whether the dataset is intended to contain every corporate action of every BSE-listed equity in a period, and whether you document how completeness is assured. In particular, can a period with no record for a company be taken to mean the company had no corporate action of that type in that period?
4. **Fields:** whether records separate announcement date, record date, ex-date and effective date; whether they carry ratios and terms, ISIN (old and new where applicable), BSE scrip code, and a unique event identifier; and whether original notices or circulars are referenced or linked.
5. **Provenance and upstream sources:** how the data is compiled (for example from company intimations to BSE under the SEBI listing regulations), and whether any part is sourced from other exchanges, depositories or third parties. We specifically need to know whether any part derives from NSE data.
6. **Samples:** whether a sample file and the record schema can be shared for a few companies, for example Torrent Pharmaceuticals, GAIL (India) and HDFC Bank for calendar year 2022, or a schema document if samples are not possible.
7. **Terms:** subscription and licensing terms for historical corporate-action data for internal research use only (no redistribution), including any one-time historical purchase option, current prices, and restrictions on storage and use.

Thank you.

Regards,
[Owner name and organisation]
